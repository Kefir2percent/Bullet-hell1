SO... HOW DOES THIS WORK?

HOW TO PLAY

A boss, a garden, and questionable life choices. Here's the deal.

👋

WELCOME TO THE GARDEN

Your mission is pretty simple: run around, fight the boss, try not to die, and get the biggest score you can. Easy, right? Probably not.

🕹️
THE BASICS

CONTROLS

WASD

Move around. Touch grass, literally.

↑←↓→

Also works for moving, if that's your thing.

SPACE

Attack the boss. Make it regret everything.

ESC

Pause the game. Take a deep breath.

Psst! These are example controls. Update them if your game uses different keys.

🥬
WHAT'S GOING ON?

GAME MECHANICS

  • Move around the garden. It's not going to explore itself.
  • Find the boss and start the fight.
  • Hit the boss to make its HP go down.
  • Try not to get hit too much. You're not invincible.
  • Earn points and see how high you can climb on the leaderboard.
🎉
THE GOOD ENDING

HOW TO WIN

BOSS DEFEATED ✓
  • Get the boss's HP down to zero.
  • Stay alive long enough to finish the job.
  • Congratulations! You actually did it.
  • Your boss status becomes YES.
  • Enjoy your score. Try to get on the Top 5 list!
💀
WELL, THAT HAPPENED

HOW TO LOSE

OOPS... GAME OVER
  • Run out of lives before defeating the boss.
  • Stare at the screen for a moment. It's okay.
  • Your boss status becomes NO.
  • Your final score is recorded, if score saving is enabled.
  • Try again. The boss isn't going to beat itself.
💡

PRO TIP (TOTALLY LEGIT)

Don't stand still and let the boss turn you into garden decoration. Keep moving, keep attacking, and maybe — just maybe — you'll survive.
