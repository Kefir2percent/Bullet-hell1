WHAT'S GOING ON?

GAME MECHANICS

  • Score is crucial during the game-time. Try not to waste 'em beforehand
  • You get short I-Frame duration after death, and get 25% of score to be cut off
  • Bombs clear dense bullet patterns. To use a bomb, you must have a score of at least 10,000. If your score drops below 10,000, you cannot use bombs. Each bomb costs 2,000 points and is activated by pressing X.
  • During the fight, you have 25 lives in total.
